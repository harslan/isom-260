Who made it

Open source, from Hugging Face 🤗

🤗 Hugging Face

The "GitHub of AI." Open models, datasets, and apps anyone can use. They bought Pollen Robotics, the team that builds Reachy Mini.

📱 Its app store = Hugging Face Spaces

Apps are shared as Spaces. One click installs a new skill: talk, hand tracking, radio...

🐍 You can build for it

Python or a simple web app. There is a free simulator, so you don't even need the robot to start.

💵 What it costs

About $399 (plugs into a laptop) to $499 (wireless). More than 10,000 sold.
