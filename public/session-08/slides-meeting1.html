The analogy for today

The food truck and the kitchen.

🚚

Food truck

one person

Takes the order, cooks, and holds the cash.

Fast. Cheap. Usually exactly right.

🍽️

Restaurant kitchen

🛎️ host🧾 server🧑‍🍳 cook✅ expediter💵 cashier

Same food. More people. Every one of them is there for a reason.

Nobody hires an expediter "because it felt complicated."

A food truck that hires five people for a slow Tuesday goes broke. More agents is more bill, not more intelligence.

The only honest rule: name the reason

Four reasons to split. That's the whole list.

💵
Permissions
Only the cashier opens the register.
Only one part can press send or refund.
✅
Specialization
The expediter checks every plate and never cooks.
A checker that never writes what it checks.
🧾
Isolation
The server takes orders from strangers all night and doesn't carry the keys to the safe.
The part that reads strangers holds no buttons.
🔥
Parallelism
Saturday night: ten cooks, ten tables.
Many workers at once, one boss merges.

Not on the list: "it felt complicated""more agents = smarter""the vendor said 12 agents"

What the room just proved

Rules make it likely. Walls make it certain.

Fooling the Reader only puts a ticket on the rail it holds no button. The thief needs a second thing to go wrong, and the second thing is a person
🟡 is not a failure. It's the design the AI can't tell whether a customer is lying. Your register, your receipts, your order log can. That's permissions
The Writer never saw the thief's message only the ticket. If it isn't on the ticket, the cook never hears it
One idea to take into any job

Never put all three in one box.

📨
Reads strangers
emails, web pages, résumés, uploaded files
🗄️
Sees private data
customer records, payments, your inbox
📤
Can act outside
send, refund, post, pay

Your food truck read strangers and held a money button. Give it customer records and it has all three. The kitchen splits them up. That's isolation.

Security researcher Simon Willison calls this combination "the lethal trifecta" (2025). Every shop you robbed today had two of the three.

AI for BUSINESS · the five questions for any AI vendor

Before you buy a team of agents.

"Why is it split?" a reason per switch: permissions, specialization, isolation, parallelism. Or admit it's decoration
"What passes between the stations?" show us one ticket. If it's "a prompt," it's shouting across the kitchen
"Who checks, and did they write it?" a checker that wrote the draft is not a checker
"Which part reads strangers, and what can it press?" the right answer is "nothing"
"What do 1,000 messages cost?" your Heist Report has the number. One robbery pays for months of kitchen
