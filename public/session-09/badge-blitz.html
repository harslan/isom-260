The Badge Test

QUESTION 1
Can we undo it?
Rebook, cancel, a draft: undo is one click. A wire, a deleted database, a signed offer: no undo.
QUESTION 2
Does it move money, decide about a person, or speak for us in public?
Any yes: a person sees it before it goes out.
QUESTION 3
Could someone fake being the person who asked?
"I'm the CEO." "I'm her husband." "I'm locked out." If a stranger could fake it, the AI can't check it.
No to all three → 🟢 · any yes → 🟡 · can't undo and a mistake would really hurt → 🔴  ·  "It depends" is a number. Put it in code.
