Secret #1 · tokens

AI doesn't read words. It reads tokens.

A token ≈ ¾ of an English word a compressed chunk from a ~100,000-piece vocabulary
Your TinyGPT's tokens were single letters same idea, vocabulary of 27
Every API bill, rate limit, and context window is measured in tokens
LIVE: let's tokenize things → (tokenizer on screen)

Demo cue: platform.openai.com/tokenizer — paste a sentence, an emoji, a non-English sentence.

The famous failure

Why AI couldn't count the r's in 🍓 strawberry.

The model never sees s-t-r-a-w-b-e-r-r-y it sees [straw][berry], two opaque chunks
Letter-counting through token-glasses is like counting bricks through frosted glass
Modern reasoning models learned workarounds but the lesson stands: models perceive tokens, not characters
Business version the same effect makes some LANGUAGES cost 2–3× more tokens per sentence. Budget accordingly.
Secret #2 · the window

The window is the whole memory.

Your TinyGPT: 16 letters you saw block_size=16 in the code. Nothing before it existed.
Frontier models: 200K–1M tokens same rule, bigger window
Why chatbots 'forget' old turns fall out of the window. Gone is gone.
And you're billed for the WHOLE window on every call long chats get expensive (remember cache reads: −75%)

"Just paste all our documents in" fails at scale — the real fix is retrieval (RAG), Session 7.
