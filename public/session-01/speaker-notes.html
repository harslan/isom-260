—Notebook Act 1 walkthrough (stay off slides)1:18 · 12 min

These are the exact outputs everyone will see — the notebook is seeded (random.seed(260)), so the whole room gets identical "random" results. Use that as a beat.

Cell: load the data (~30 sec)
Loaded 32,033 names. First 10: ['emma', 'olivia', 'ava', 'isabella', 'sophia', ...]

"32,033 real names. This is our 'internet' — the entire world our model will ever know. Hold that thought for later."

Cell: count letter pairs (~2 min)
After 'q', the next letter is usually: 'u' (76%), '.' (10%), 'a' (5%) ... Names most often START with: 'a' (14%), 'k' (9%), 'm' (8%) ...

"Nobody told it Q goes with U. Nobody programmed a rule. It counted. This is the core move of all machine learning: patterns in, behavior out. The difference between this and GPT-6 is scale, not philosophy."

Ask the room: "Shout a letter — let's interrogate the model." Change top_next('x') to their letter, re-run. 30 seconds of delight, builds the 'I can poke it' instinct.

Cell: generate names (~3 min) — the payoff
✨ 15 names invented by the counting model: Za · Khini · Ahusenyaiy · Molvian · Tanana · Jaza · Nerreth ...

"Look at these. 'Tanana' — almost a name! 'Ahusenyaiy' — a keyboard falling down stairs. Why the range? (fish for it) The model has ONE letter of memory. Picking letter six, it only remembers letter five. It can't know it already used four vowels. Remember the restaurant demo inventing statistics? Same disease: generating plausible-next-thing with no grounding. You're looking at the embryo of a hallucination."

The seed beat: "Compare with your neighbor. ... Identical, right? 'Random' in computing is often seeded and reproducible — we set seed 260, for the course number. That reproducibility instinct will matter when you're testing agents later."

Cell: temperature (~3 min)
😴 T=0.5 (cautious) → An, Ma, Ja, Anay, Bri 🤪 T=2.0 (chaotic) → Kyeaiann, Kmek, Faydovinerresnylenmysa

"One knob: temperature. Low — safe, boring, repetitive. High — creative and unhinged. 'Faydovinerresnylenmysa' is what an LLM sounds like with the safety off. This exact knob, same name, is in the real APIs you'll use in Session 3. Quick business instinct check: contract summarization — high or low? Brainstorming campaign names? You now have a tuning intuition most working professionals don't."

Hard stop 1:30

Stop before Act 2 even if ahead — the cliffhanger is the design. "Everything past this cell is tonight's homework, and it's the good part."
