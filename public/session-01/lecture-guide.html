Part 2 · The Landscape

The State of AI in Business, September 2026

Forget the hype cycle. Here are the numbers that describe the world you're about to graduate into — and the gap hiding inside them.

91%
of businesses use AI in at least one capacity — up from 78% in 2024. Using AI is no longer a differentiator. It's the floor.
79%
of companies are adopting AI agents — software that doesn't just answer questions, but takes actions to get work done.
23%
…but only 23% are scaling agents in even one business function. And 40%+ of agentic projects get cancelled before they ship value.
80%
of enterprise apps shipped in Q1 2026 embed at least one AI agent. In 2024 that number was 33%. Agents went from feature to default in two years.
31%
of enterprises have an agent in production — with banking and insurance leading the pack at 47%. Regulated industries are moving fastest, not slowest.
5.1 mo
median time-to-value on agent deployments. Not weeks. Making AI actually pay off takes real work — and real people who know how.
The gap is the opportunity
The gap between "adopting" (79%) and "scaling" (23%) is where careers are made. Companies don't need more AI hype — they need people who can make AI actually work. That's this course.

Part 5 · Hands-On

Hands-On: Train Your Own Tiny LLM

Here's the secret nobody tells you on day one: every LLM — from the 100,000-parameter toy you're about to train, to GPT-6 Astra with its "generational leap" — does the same thing. It predicts the next token. Everything else is scale.

Today, you train one yourself. In the browser. For free. In minutes.
Act 1: A model made of counting
We start with the dumbest possible language model — literally counting which letters follow which. It already invents plausible new names. That should bother you a little.
Act 2: A real transformer
Then we train an actual 100K-parameter transformer — the same architecture behind every frontier model — and watch it get noticeably better at the same job.
Ready? Open your laptop.
Runs entirely in Google Colab — nothing to install, nothing to pay for. If you can use Google Docs, you can train a language model.

Part 6 · Discussion

Where Did AI Already Touch Your Morning?

Before you'd even had coffee today, a dozen AI systems had already made decisions on your behalf. Let's count them.

In-class exercise · Pairs · 5 minutes
No wrong answers, only missed ones
With the person next to you, list every AI touchpoint from the moment you woke up to the moment you sat down in this room. Think small:
  • Your feed — every post you saw was ranked by a model
  • Maps routing you around traffic on the way to campus
  • The spam that never reached your inbox
  • Autocomplete finishing your texts
  • Face unlock deciding you are you
  • The Spotify queue that somehow knew your mood
Pairs with the longest list share out. Most people find 10+ once they start looking.
The point
AI is not coming — it's ambient. It already shapes what you see, hear, buy, and believe. The question this semester isn't whether AI will be part of your career. It's who directs it — you, or someone else.
