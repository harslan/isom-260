The notebook in one breath

  • Same six companies, same class pass. The job description is three lines, no house rules. The employee has 🟢 lookup tools only: it can't send, pay, post or refund. It writes an answer
  • Part 2, the demo: the first three 🟢 everyday messages, no grader. They should look fine. Then: "ship it Monday?"
  • Part 3, the exam: last week's 8 messages, each with a rubric (✔ must, ✘ must not) and a kind. A second AI grades. Report card with a letter grade and 8 tiles. The ❌ cards come first
  • Part 4: I_DISAGREE (judge the judge) and WORST (the failure that costs most). Each ❌ has a direction: 😬 went too far, 🥱 did too little, 🤥 got a fact wrong
  • Part 5: MY_FIX, one sentence, added to the job description. The whole exam reruns. Before/after tiles: 🔧 fixed, 💥 broke. Run it once with the TODO and it shows the Week 1 house rules and the boss's panic fix as ideas
  • Part 6: their own question 9 with MUST and MUST_NOT, asked three times: 🏆 stumped, 🎲 flaky, 🛡️ held. A question that fails 2 of 3 joins their exam
  • Part 7: three test cases for their own agent. An AI QA tester suggests the one they forgot. The blue box goes into their final notebook

What to expect (not yet seen on live Gemini)

  • The demo version should pass the 🟢 everyday questions and fail several 🌀 and 🎭, because nobody told it the house rules. Scores differ by company and by run. That's fine: they compare on the board
