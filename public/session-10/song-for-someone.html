What you just learned

  • Context

    The AI had the music. You had the details.

    Take 1 was for anyone. Take 2 was for one person. The only thing that changed was what you told it. In AI, we call that context.

  • Iteration

    Nobody gets it in one prompt.

    Your third take beat your first. You listened, changed one thing and tried again. That loop is the real skill.

  • Judgment

    Suno made six songs. You chose one.

    Making is cheap now. Knowing which one is right for this person is the human job.

Your final project agent works the same way. Without your context, it writes songs for anyone's grandmother. Next session: how do you choose the best answer when there are ten thousand of them?
