S0 · The Question

One Sentence Through the Transformer

You type this. A trillion-parameter machine fills the blank in 40 milliseconds. What actually happens in there? Six stops. No math. One sentence.

Say it like a pro "A language model is a machine that turns context into a probability for every possible next word."
S1 · Tokens

First, the sentence gets chopped

Models don't read letters or words — they read tokens, chunks of roughly ¾ of a word. Every token is just an ID in a big lookup table. Cost, context limits, and the model's weird quirks are all measured in tokens.

Say it like a pro "First, text is tokenized — chopped into chunks the model can look up."
S2 · Embeddings

Every token becomes a place on a map

In an embedding, meaning is location — remember your vowel map! Words used alike live near each other. But "bank" was seen next to rivers and next to loans, so its stored point is the average of every "bank" it ever read — parked in limbo between RIVER-LAND and MONEY-LAND.

Say it like a pro "Each token becomes a point in meaning-space — and an ambiguous word like 'bank' starts between its meanings."
S3 · Attention — the hero move

The other words pull "bank" home

Attention is a negotiation: every word asks "who's relevant to me?", every other word offers what it has, and strong matches pull your meaning into the right neighborhood. Here, interest matches hardest (0.55) — and drags bank into MONEY-LAND.

Say it like a pro "Attention lets each word's meaning be rewritten by the words around it — 'interest' pulls 'bank' into finance."

S4 · Layers

One pass fixes a word. Stacked passes build meaning.

One attention pass fixed "bank". Now stack that move: Layer 1 fixes words, Layer 2 builds phrases, deeper layers compress the whole thing into one sentence-level idea. Your TinyGPT does this 2 times. GPT-6 class models: 100+.

Say it like a pro "Layers repeat attention-then-think dozens of times — each pass a deeper reading of the same sentence."
S5 · The Prediction

Meaning becomes a bet

The final position becomes a score for every token in the vocabulary; softmax turns scores into probabilities; temperature decides how boldly it bets; it samples one token, appends it, and repeats — one word at a time, forever.

Say it like a pro "The model outputs probabilities for every possible next token, samples one, and repeats — that's the whole show."
