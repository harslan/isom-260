Part 1 · The Metaphor

THE MEETING

At every position, every letter joins a meeting with everything that came before it. Three things happen — every time, at every position, in every layer.

query
🙋

ASK

Every letter broadcasts a question: "I'm an i in position 4 — who's relevant to me?"

key
📢

OFFER

Every earlier letter advertises what it has: "I'm an h, right before you."

value
🎁

CONTRIBUTE

The good matches hand over their information, weighted by relevance.

Relevance scores → softmax → a weighted blend. That's the entire mechanism. Multiply by 4 heads × 2 layers and you have your model; by 100+ layers and you have GPT-6.
