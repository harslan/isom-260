What the raters did to the models

Every quirk of the raters became a quirk of the machine.

Sycophancy raters rewarded agreement → models became flatterers (labs now train against it)
Length bias raters rewarded longer answers → chatbots pad, and you pay per token
Over-refusal raters over-rewarded caution → models refused legitimate work; now recalibrated
The lesson alignment is a values problem wearing a math costume

2026's big shift · test-time compute

The second dial: thinking longer.

Scaling used to mean one thing: a bigger model more parameters, more data, more months of training
Reasoning models added a second dial: more thinking per question the model drafts, checks, and revises before answering — "test-time compute"
You control that dial — and pay for it low effort: fast & cheap for routine work · max effort: slow, expensive, and how GPT-6 hit its benchmark numbers
Why transformers made all this possible attention runs in parallel on GPUs — the architecture that scales is the architecture that wins

Business rule of thumb: match thinking time to decision stakes. An invoice-classifier shouldn't ponder; a contract-risk review should.
