What am I looking at?
  • This is ONE artificial neuron: a weighted vote plus a threshold. Multiply each input by a weight, add a bias, approve if the total clears zero. That's it. That's the atom of AI.
  • "Learning" = nudging the weights downhill on the loss. When you press the green button, the machine repeats a tiny move — which direction makes me less wrong? — exactly what you watched your TinyGPT do 2,500 times in Session 1.
  • Scale is the only difference. Your TinyGPT is 104,475 of these knobs. GPT-6 is trillions. Same idea, same downhill walk.
